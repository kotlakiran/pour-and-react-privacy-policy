Pour & React: 3D Science Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Pour & React: 3D Science Lab ("the app") is a puzzle and learning game developed by Kiran Kotla. This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to all users, including children.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not request any Android runtime permissions and does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (stars, current levels, streaks, achievements, journal entries) and settings (sound, haptics, voice guide) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves the device. It is not backed up by the app, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Reset progress" in Settings.

Voice guide

The optional voice guide reads instructions aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app sends text to that engine only; it does not record or listen to audio.

Children

The app is suitable for children. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, no personal information is collected from children. Science experiments shown in the app include adult-supervision safety notes; real-world experiments should only be done with a grown-up.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
